# Pulse Investment Platform - Signup & Login Guide

## For New Users - Creating Your Account

### Step 1: Go to Signup Page
Visit: **https://pulse-investment-platform-design-qsmkgpbja.vercel.app/auth/sign-up**

### Step 2: Fill Your Details
- **Full Name**: Your name (e.g., "Jane Nkosi")
- **Email**: A valid email address you check regularly
- **Password**: At least 6 characters (mix of letters, numbers, symbols for security)

### Step 3: Click "Create account"
- The form will validate your entries
- If there's an issue, you'll see an error message

### Step 4: Check Your Email
You'll see a confirmation page that says:
```
Account created!
We've sent a confirmation email to your inbox. Click the link in the email to verify your account.
```

**Important Steps:**
1. Check your email inbox (including SPAM/Junk folder)
2. Find the email from Pulse with subject line about confirming your email
3. Click the confirmation link in the email
4. You'll be redirected to confirm your email

### Step 5: Return and Sign In
After confirming your email:
1. Go to **https://pulse-investment-platform-design-qsmkgpbja.vercel.app/auth/login**
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click "Sign in"
4. You'll be taken to your Pulse dashboard!

## For Existing Users - Signing In

1. Go to: **https://pulse-investment-platform-design-qsmkgpbja.vercel.app/auth/login**
2. Enter your email address
3. Enter your password
4. Click "Sign in"

### If You Get "Email not confirmed" Error
This means you haven't verified your email yet. Here's what to do:

1. Check your email inbox (including Spam/Junk)
2. Find the Pulse confirmation email
3. Click the confirmation link
4. Return to the login page and try again

## Troubleshooting

### I didn't receive a confirmation email

**Solutions:**
- Check your Spam/Junk folder - confirmation emails sometimes go there
- Wait 5 minutes - emails can take a few moments to arrive
- Try signing up with a different email address
- Contact support if the problem persists

### The confirmation link isn't working

**This could happen if:**
- The link has expired (links are valid for 24 hours)
- You've already confirmed this email
- There's a technical issue

**Solutions:**
- Try signing up again with the same email
- Use a different email address
- Contact support for assistance

### I can't sign in even after confirming my email

**Possible reasons:**
- You're using the wrong password
- Your email address is different from the one you signed up with
- Your account hasn't fully confirmed yet

**Solutions:**
1. Double-check your email and password (including CAPS LOCK)
2. Try creating a new account with your correct email
3. Contact support if issues persist

### I forgot my password

Currently, we don't have a "forgot password" feature. Please:
1. Try signing up again with your email
2. Contact support for password recovery

## Dashboard Access

Once you're signed in, you'll see:
- **Portfolio Overview** - Your total balance and investments
- **Navigation Menu** - Access different features:
  - **Home** - Dashboard overview
  - **Invest** - Browse available projects
  - **Sale** - Manage your positions
  - **Stake** - Stake your tokens
  - **Wallet** - Manage funds
  - **Signals** - Market insights
  - **Profile** - Your account settings and KYC

---

## KYC Verification (Optional Initially)

When you first access the dashboard, you may see a KYC (Know Your Customer) verification banner. This is optional at first, but required to:
- Deposit funds
- Make investments
- Withdraw returns

KYC takes ~5 minutes and requires:
- Personal information
- Government ID photo
- Proof of address

You can complete this anytime from your Profile section.
