## Step 2: Configure Professional Domain (Background Work)

**This happens in the background while users are registering.**

### The 2-Minute Setup

1. **Log into your domain registrar**
   - GoDaddy, Namecheap, Bluehost, 1&1, HostGator, etc.
   - See DNS_QUICK_START.md for your specific registrar

2. **Go to DNS settings for pulseinvestme.com**
   - Find "Manage DNS" or "DNS Settings"
   - Look for "Host Records" or "DNS Records"

3. **Add ONE A Record**
   - Type: **A**
   - Name/Host: Leave blank (or @)
   - Value: **76.76.21.21**
   - TTL: Leave default
   - Click Save

4. **Done!**
   - You just configured professional DNS
   - Takes 2 minutes
   - The rest is automatic

### That's All You Do

Seriously. One A record. That's it.
